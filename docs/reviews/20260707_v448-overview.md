## 1. Executive summary

v421–v448 shipped **twenty-eight slices** across two distinct phases. Slices v421–v447 were dominated by a comprehensive asset pipeline overhaul — hero, weapon, monster, and gear rigs; bone gear sockets; new GLBs; attack animations; dungeon room corridor PCG; wall occlusion fade — that required minimal server or protocol changes but substantially reshaped the client presentation layer. Slice v448 returned to gameplay with the quest steward hunt loop: a depth-scoped trophy hunt resolving into deterministic family-offer rewards.

**Architecture held.** Server authority, protocol v8, determinism invariants, and intent-registry discipline are intact across 28 slices. New gameplay domains (unique effects, set items, quest steward) all followed the established micro-module pattern. Zero `helpers=globals()` extraction coupling sites.

**Notable gain:** Today's `skill_rules_validation.go` extraction dropped `rules.go` by 464 lines (3,237 → 2,783) — the largest single rules-file reduction since the v414 survival delegation. The 16 extracted validators are independently callable and unit-tested.

The pattern of adding to coordinators rather than extracting to focused modules continued across every one of the 28 slices.

**v420 follow-ups:** One high-priority finding closed (skill validation extraction landed today). Three findings still open (main.gd _apply_delta, inventory_panel.gd split, validate_shared.cross_checks() decomposition). One new known issue surfaced: `TestDungeonTeleportersReplayGolden` is flaky due to navBudget exhaustion introduced between v420 and v448.

## 3. Cross-cutting themes

1. **Asset pipeline dominated the window, then gameplay resumed cleanly.** Twenty-four of the 28 slices (v421–v447, minus v447 class-gear-retune and v448 quest steward) were asset pipeline work using the spec-gate exemption correctly. The four gameplay slices (v426 unique effects, v427 set items, v447 class gear retune, v448 steward hunt) each arrived with full specs, plans, and as-builts.

2. **Coordinator debt is stable but accelerating in main.gd.** `main.gd` grew 86 lines in 28 slices (vs +15 in the prior 12-slice batch). The rate has increased even though most slices were presentation-only — the `_apply_delta` fan-out hub grows with every new client event type regardless of feature complexity. This is the steepest trend in the codebase.

3. **New gameplay domains landed as focused modules.** Quest steward, unique effects, and set items all followed the micro-module pattern: dedicated `*_rules.go` validators, focused handler files, companion tests. The pattern is working for new domains.

4. **Extraction ceiling pressure building in multiple files.** `sim.go` is at its ratchet ceiling (6,635). `stash_panel.gd` is at exactly baseline+25 (1,180). `inventory_panel.gd` is 11 lines above its baseline (1,527 vs 1,516). `run.py` is at its ceiling (4,438). Any substantive addition to any of these files requires either a real extraction or a justified baseline advance.

5. **`TestDungeonTeleportersReplayGolden` flakiness is a hidden CI quality debt.** The test was passing at v420 and is now intermittently failing due to pathfinding budget exhaustion in town navigation. Because it is an extended-only test (not in the fast CI pack), the regression went undetected across 28 slices. The fix is well-scoped (increase navBudget or remove town navigation from the replay scaffold) but documents a gap in extended-scenario CI fidelity.

## 4. Top 10 recommendations

Tags: **[blocker]** = before next feature batch; **[minor]** = safe `$refactor`; **[future]** = multi-slice.

1. **[future · Maint]** Extract `main.gd` `_apply_delta` op-dispatch hub (`main.gd:1286–1742`, 456 lines) into a `DeltaEventCoordinator` module. The fan-out loop is the client's most brittle seam; a sequence-order bug or protocol rename will silently break unrelated features.

2. **[future · Maint]** Extract `applyMovement` and `advanceMonsterAttack` from `sim.go` into dedicated phase files. `sim.go` is at its ratchet ceiling (6,635); the next combat slice will breach it.

3. **[future · Maint]** Decompose `validate_shared.cross_checks()` (~2,941 lines in one function) into named sub-functions (`_check_monster_refs`, `_check_skill_stat_cross`, `_check_unique_item_refs`, etc.). Internal structure, not file size, is the problem.

4. **[future · Maint]** Begin `inventory_panel.gd` split (1,527 lines, 11 above ratchet baseline). First step: extract `_build_item_tooltip_*` cluster into `inventory_tooltip_builder.gd`.

5. **[future · Maint]** Decompose `stash_panel.gd` (at exactly baseline+25, 1,180 lines) before breach. Extract shared `ItemSlotRenderer` helper also used by `shop_panel.gd` and `market_panel.gd`.

6. **[minor · Correctness]** Add `quest_steward.v0.json` cross-checks to `validate_shared`: trophy monster_def_id, item_def_id, and reward template_ids all validated against their respective catalogs.

7. **[minor · Maint]** Extract `quest_steward_pick` action from `run.py` into `tools/bot/quest_runtime.py`, following the `movement_runtime.py` pattern. Paired `test_quest_runtime.py` proves extraction independence.

8. **[minor · Test]** Fix `TestDungeonTeleportersReplayGolden` flakiness — increase navBudget for post-teleport town traversal or remove navigation from the replay scaffold. Pre-existing regression from v421–v448 dungeon layout changes.

9. **[minor · SDD]** Write ADR for the quest system. Steward hunt introduced permanent design decisions (trophy depth-scoping, deterministic family-offer generation, `quest_source_depth` wire field, turn-in pipeline) that will constrain all future quest and NPC interaction types.

10. **[minor · Test]** Complete v448 deferred `quest_steward_pick_intent` end-to-end Godot client CI proof — a pinned-seed `99_steward_family_pick.json` client scenario extending `98_steward_hunt_quest`.
