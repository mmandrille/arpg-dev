## 1. Executive summary

v449–v460 shipped **twelve gameplay-focused slices** after the v421–v448 asset pipeline marathon. The window covered status effects, build-defining uniques and affinity passives, account resource wallet bag, a four-slice combat stability chain (session transport, damage burst events, skill budget, six-player soak), live Godot transport proof, skill ground aim, and leveled potions.

**Architecture held at the authority boundary.** Server owns outcomes; protocol v8 extended in-place; determinism invariants green through step 7 of CI. The **v453 realtime extraction** (`fanout_coalesce.go`, `persist_defer.go`, etc.) is the largest structural win — genuinely decoupled modules with zero extraction-coupling debt.

**Coordinator debt accelerated.** `sim.go` grew 147 lines with the ratchet ceiling **advanced** (6,635 → 6,782) instead of phase extraction. `bot_controller.gd` hit headroom 0. `_apply_delta` event fan-out grew to 472 lines despite wallet-runtime extraction on the changes loop. `run.py` grew +88 lines to its ceiling.

**New correctness gaps surfaced.** `OpResourceBagItemAdd` is not actor-scoped in `filterChangesForClient` (co-op leak). `PotionIconLabel` hardcodes potion IDs (data-driven policy violation). `status_effects.v0.json` is not validated by `validate_shared`. Leveled potion restore formulas lack cross-language golden coverage above level 1.

**Process hygiene slipped.** v454–v456 skipped plans; v458 has no as-built; scenario catalog is ~14 entries stale. ADR-0016 and ADR-0017 are positive counterweights.

## 3. Cross-cutting themes

1. **Combat stability chain landed real architecture.** v453–v456 delivered deferred persist, fanout coalescing, skill budget, and soak proofs. The realtime package is healthier than at v448.

2. **Ratchet advances replaced extractions.** `sim.go`, `run.py`, `handlers.go`, and `rules.go` all advanced baselines in this window. The maintainability ratchet is being used as a release valve rather than a forcing function for extraction.

3. **New domains follow the micro-module pattern; coordinators still absorb wiring.** Status effects, potions, resource bag, skill budget — all focused Go files. Client wiring (`main.gd` events loop, `inventory_panel.gd`) grew faster than extractions.

4. **Validation lagged feature velocity.** Status effects catalog shipped v449 but `validate_shared` never learned to load it. Leveled potions shipped v460 without golden extension. These are merge-risk gaps fixable in `$refactor`.

5. **Live Godot transport proof is a durable CI pattern.** v457's `runner: godot_client` + reconnect assertion should be the template for any future latency/transport regression.
