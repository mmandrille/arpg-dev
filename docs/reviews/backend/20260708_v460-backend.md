## Summary

v449–v460 returned to sustained gameplay and combat-system work after the v421–v448 asset-heavy window. The standout architectural gain is **v453 combat session stability**: realtime persist/fanout logic extracted into focused modules (`fanout_coalesce.go`, `enqueue_coalesce.go`, `persist_batch.go`, `persist_defer.go`) with zero `helpers=globals()` coupling. New gameplay domains (status effects, resource bag, leveled potions, skill budget/burst) follow the micro-module pattern with dedicated handler registrations.

Structural debt **worsened in `sim.go`**: the ratchet ceiling was advanced from 6,635 to 6,782 (+147 lines) rather than extracting `applyMovement` / `advanceMonsterAttack` phase bodies. A co-op correctness gap surfaced: `OpResourceBagItemAdd` is persisted in `persistTick` but **not actor-scoped** in `filterChangesForClient` (`session_loop.go:627–646`), so resource-bag grants may leak to non-owning clients in co-op.

ADR-0016 (combat processing budget) and ADR-0017 (skill aim/cast resolution) landed. Quest system ADR remains unwritten from v448.

## 1. Architecture

**[Strength]** Realtime extraction quartet (v453) is genuinely decoupled. `fanout_coalesce.go:8` (`clientFanoutBatch`), `enqueue_coalesce.go:7` (`clientSendOverflow`), and `persist_defer.go` are independently testable with typed interfaces — the model for future extractions.

**[Strength]** Intent registry discipline held. Six new intents registered via single `inputHandlers` map lines in `handlers.go:26+`; `applyInput` in `sim.go` untouched with new `case` blocks.

**[Strength]** Deferred persist policy (`session_tick.go:57`, `session_loop.go:13`) protects the 10 Hz tick budget without sacrificing correctness — deferred changes flush before the next tick processes dependent inputs.

**[Strength]** New domain modules under 600 lines: `status_effects.go`, `potion_items.go`, `resource_bag.go`, `skill_budget.go`, `skill_damage_burst.go`, `skill_aim_nav.go`.

**[High]** `sim.go` phase bodies remain in-file at 6,782 lines. `applyMovement` (~`sim.go:2871`), `advanceMonsterAttack` (~`sim.go:3316`), `advanceProjectiles` (~`sim.go:3696`) account for ~825 lines. Ceiling was **advanced**, not resolved.

**[High]** `OpResourceBagItemAdd` co-op broadcast leak. `filterChangesForClient` (`session_loop.go:630–635`) actor-scopes inventory, stash, gold, wallet ops but **omits** `OpResourceBagItemAdd`. `resource_bag.go:67` emits the change without `OwnerPlayerID`. Solo play is harmless; co-op may show phantom bag entries on non-owning clients until snapshot reconciliation.

**[Med]** `handleCastSkill` instant-projectile dispatch has redundant nested guards (`handlers.go:1225–1238`). `skillUsesInstantResolution` (`skill_damage_burst.go:99`) and inner `ArrowCount > 0` checks overlap; fragile if resolution rules change.

**[Med]** `repos.go` grew to 2,369 (+65) with resource-bag transfer methods added inline rather than routing through `resource_bag_store.go` partitioning.
